Heavy Irons — Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Heavy Irons is a local-only app. Your training lives on your phone — nowhere else. This page explains that in plain language, because there is very little to explain.

01 What stays on your device

All of your workout data is stored only on your device. Nothing you log is uploaded, synced, or backed up by Heavy Irons.

  • Sessions
  • Sets
  • Reps
  • Weights
  • Notes
  • Body weight entries

The app works fully offline. It never needs an internet connection to record or show your training.

02 What we collect — nothing

The developer collects no data of any kind.

No accounts
There are no user accounts to create or sign into.
No servers
Heavy Irons has no servers — there is nowhere for data to go.
No tracking
No analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.
No sharing
No data is sold, shared, or sent to any third party.

03 Backups & deletion

iPhone backups may include the app’s data as part of your device backup. That backup is made by iOS and is controlled entirely by your own Apple settings — for example, whether you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup — not by Heavy Irons.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data permanently. There is no copy on a server to recover, because there never was one.

04 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
